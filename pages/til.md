# Today I learned
***Stability is an Illusion***

We all love the feeling of a 'solid ground' under our feet. We plan our vacations, our careers, and our lives as if the world is a static, predictable place. But if the last few years have taught us anything, it’s that stability is an illusion.

Look at the global landscape. In 2022, the world as we knew it shifted overnight. Politics and safety became uncertain. And just recently, the events of February 28th reminded us once again. One moment you have everything — a plan, a routine, a sense of security — and the next, pieces of your life start falling away.

**So nothing is permanent.**

What do we do? We can’t control the world, but we can calibrate our internal compass. The goal isn’t to find a stable world, but to build a flexible nervous system. We need to learn how to adapt, how to breathe through the chaos, and how to find a center within ourselves when everything outside is spinning.

Don't wait for the world to settle down — it won't. Instead, learn to dance on shifting ground. Stay fluid, stay brave, and remember: you are not defined by what stays, but by how you handle what goes.

*Thank you.*
